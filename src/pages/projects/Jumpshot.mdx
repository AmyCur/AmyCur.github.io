## Brief

Create an adventure style prototype for a brand new 2.5D Platformer game, targeted for console and PC release. 

## About

This project was developed for my Level 3 BTEC in Games Design. In this project, you play as a character with a gun that shoots ricocheting bullets; if these bullets bounce off of a wall and then hit you, you will peform a double jump. The player also has a dash and using these two movement abilities, they must figure out how they can over come the obsticles that are in their way.
