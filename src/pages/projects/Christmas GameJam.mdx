## Brief
To create as christmas themed game around the theme of "Gift Giving"

## Description
This is a 3D story based walking simulator where you play the role of an elf who delivers presents to other elves.
Eventually, you deliver a present to an elf which contains a bomb, revealing that Santa was evil. You must then 
place a bomb of your own in order to defeat santa.

This game was created in a team of **5 people**.
