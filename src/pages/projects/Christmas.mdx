## Brief

To create a game around the theme of gift giving

## About

I worked in a team of 5 people in order to create a christmas themed game where you walk around santas workshop and give elfs gifts. 

It is split into two days; on the first day, you hand out gifts as usual. However, on the 2nd day the player delivers a gift that contains a bomb, which santa planted, revealing his evil plan.

You must then fight santa, using dynamite to kill him
