## Brief
To create a game set within the Meta Menaces universe, in a specific time period of our choice.

## Description
This game was developed for the client **Iconika** and is based on their [Meta Menaces](https://themetamenaces.io/) series.
It is a 3D Movement FPS where you travel from room to room killing enemies, until you reach the final room and defeat the main villain of the Meta Menaces, **Malachai.**

This game was created in a team of **4 people**.
